GeoPulse – Data Analysis Preparation

1. Understanding EDA

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is the process of examining a dataset to understand its structure, identify patterns, find missing values, and discover useful insights.

2. Objectives of EDA

1. Understand the dataset structure.
2. Identify missing values.
3. Check duplicate records.
4. Understand data types.
5. Identify unusual or inconsistent values.
6. Discover patterns and trends.

3. Planned Analysis Steps

1. Load the cleaned dataset using Pandas.
2. Inspect rows and columns.
3. Check missing and duplicate values.
4. Understand numerical and categorical data.
5. Perform descriptive statistics.
6. Analyze relevant trends.
7. Create visualizations.
8. Prepare business insights.
